# Network+ N10-009 Study Companion

Turn a practice-test result into a specific study action. This independent companion supports CompTIA Network+ N10-009 (V9) preparation; it is not an official CompTIA product, exam-question collection or endorsement.

## Start here

- Read `STUDY-GUIDE.md` for the study routine and an objective-by-objective review checklist.
- Open `index.html` for a printable guide and links to the local companion files.
- Work through `EXERCISES.md` before opening `ANSWER-GUIDE.md`: six subnetting exercises and eight troubleshooting decisions, all using fictional evidence.
- Use `OFFICIAL-READING.md` for provider-owned references. External reading requires internet access; no official manuals or exam-objectives PDFs are bundled.
- Keep your own copy of `review-journal.csv`. Record uncertain correct answers as well as incorrect answers.
- Download `NetworkPlus-N10-009-Study-Companion-v1.zip` to keep the companion together. `manifest.json` lists the content hashes and `SHA256SUMS.txt` also covers the manifest.

## Relationship to the course

The course plan is six 90-question forms, with 90 minutes per form. This companion can be used with any available form; it does not assert that all planned forms are already available. At preparation time, Form 1 contains 90 saved questions. Use the course curriculum to see the forms currently available to you.

An 80% practice result is a study benchmark, not a conversion to CompTIA's scaled score or a guarantee of readiness. The certification exam also includes performance-based tasks. These practice decisions and paper exercises do not reproduce interactive exam tasks or replace authorized practical networking work. Check the provider's current objectives and exam policies before booking.

## Safe use and ownership

No hardware purchase, paid simulator or live-network access is required for these written exercises. Any optional practical follow-up must use an isolated network you own or are explicitly authorized to administer. Do not change shared networking equipment, capture others' traffic or scan systems without permission. The supplied outputs are fictional, not a claim of equipment testing.

The guide, exercise scenarios, worked answers and journal are original instructional material. Third-party sources remain with their respective owners and are linked, not redistributed. Read the guide alongside the sources attached to individual practice questions rather than treating this short companion as a complete networking textbook.
